Connecting Sri Lanka with Japan Through Language and Education

At GENKIMURA, we help students discover the Japanese language and prepare for exciting educational and career opportunities connected with Japan.

Welcome to GENKIMURA Japanese Language School

GENKIMURA is a Japanese language school in Sri Lanka focused on helping students learn Japanese with confidence. Our classes build practical communication skills first, so you can speak, listen, read and write in real situations.

Alongside the language, we introduce Japanese culture, manners and everyday life, because understanding people is part of understanding their language. Every student is supported at their own pace, from the first hiragana character onward.
